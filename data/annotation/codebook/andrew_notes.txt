TASK 1

"*Autre-Compteurd'eau-Résidentiel" to me this is maintenance of the water meter but I can see how it could also be considered as someone ordering a new one and therefore analoguous to bin delivery labels. 
 '*CAFÉ-TERRASSE(DOMAINEPUBLIC)' I think this one isn't the clearest. IMO it relates to café authorizations to have a terrasse on the street so does concern the neighbourhood. I should have put it in Transaction for annotation 2 though.
 '*Signalisation-Études-Personneshandicapées' This one seems to relate to putting up road signs that help people with a handicap. I see why the word Etudes could indicate internal work, but IMO this is still indicative of work happening on the street to improve the neighbourhood.
 'Appropriationcitoyenne' this is essentially citizens appropriating and improving their neighbourhood as a community so I couldn't really map it to any of the OUT criteria. 
 'Bacmontréalais(67litres)' this is analoguous to the other 'bac' labels in open coding which were labelled as IN and Transaction. 
 'Développementcommunautaire' similar to Appropriationcitoyenne
 'Grandparc' to me this is maintenance of a big park and would be IN similarly to *Bâtiment-Menuiserie being IN in open-coding
 'Mobilierurbain' to me this is maintenance of a street furniture and would be IN similarly to Mobilierdeparc being IN in open-coding
 'Sportsetloisirs-Activités' I was on the fence for this one. Felt like organizing sports and activities locally could be a neighbourhood issue but not opposed to it being considered OUT by any means.
 'Subvention-Habitation-Servicecentral' felt like a good one for the transaction category for me. But can also see this in OUT tbh
 'Surveillanceetsécuritépublique' to me this was departmental work because of the surveillance part: I would class it as being indicative of security/policing teams workload as therefore OUT. 

Suggestions: 
- clarify that public facing work that affects a specific area or a citizen in their home/neighbourhood is not internal departmental work. This is what would make bin delivery or '*Signalisation-Études-Personneshandicapées' IN whereas Direction-Finances is OUT.
- clarify the rule for objects/locations without a verb or action indicator
