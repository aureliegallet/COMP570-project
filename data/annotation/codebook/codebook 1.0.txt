===== Annotation 1: In vs Out of Scope =====

OUT: Something a resident would not recognize as a neighbourhood issue.
- Label primarily indicates a handling / collection of money
- Label appears to be a logging of a benign service request (like accessing or recording data)
- Label seems to relate to internal work within department, or merely correspondence
- Label appears to be a system log / error message
Example: "Water tax" This label describes money collection by the city in the form of a tax. It is therefore annotated as OUT as per the criteria.

IN: Something a resident would recognize as a neighbourhood issue.
- Everything else that appears to be a neighbourhood issue
Example: "Lawn Repair" This label describes a neighbourhood issue: it tracks a request for some lawn in the area to be repaired. It is therefore annotated as IN as per the criteria.

Edge case: "Transportation and delivery" This label is not very clear as to what exactly it represents. However, it could fit into two of the OUT criteria: it is either a benign service request (a citizen asking for help with transportation) or related to internal work (transport or delivery of goods handled by the city). It is therefore annotated as OUT as per the criteria.

Exclusion Rules:
